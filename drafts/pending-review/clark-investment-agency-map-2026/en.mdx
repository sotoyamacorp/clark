# Who Handles Clark Investment? CDC, CIAC and BCDA Explained

## Introduction

Companies researching Clark quickly encounter three similar acronyms: CDC, CIAC and BCDA. The confusion is practical, not cosmetic. The agency that discusses land may not be the agency that issues every operating permit or administers incentives.

The short answer is: **the first contact depends on the parcel**. Clark Freeport, the aviation complex and New Clark City have different lead entities.

## The three agencies in plain English

Put simply, **CDC runs the Clark Freeport, CIAC develops the airport-side estate, and BCDA is the parent development authority and master developer of New Clark City**.

    <tr class="border-b border-navy-100 bg-white"><td class="p-3 font-bold">BCDA</td><td class="p-3">National base-conversion authority and parent of CDC and CIAC</td><td class="p-3">New Clark City land, long-term leases, joint ventures and industrial estates</td></tr>

Executive Order No. 80 created CDC in 1993 as BCDA's operating arm for the Clark Special Economic Zone. CIAC manages the 2,367-hectare aviation complex. BCDA directly develops the 9,450-hectare New Clark City.

These figures should not be treated as a league table or simply added together. They describe different legal and management boundaries, and some Clark place names overlap.

PEZA reported in May 2026 that the broader Clark area contained five PEZA-registered ecozones with 24 locators, more than PHP62 billion in cumulative investment and over 29,000 jobs as of December 2025. However, **being in Clark and obtaining PEZA registration are not the same event**.

## Land and operating approvals may diverge

CIAC's investor guide makes the split visible. A proponent negotiates the site and lease with CIAC. After that, CIAC can endorse the locator to CDC for permits covering environmental, fire-safety, occupancy, sanitary and operating requirements.

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">A typical sequence</h3>
  <ol class="list-decimal pl-6 space-y-2 text-gray-700">
    <li>Submit the business concept and space requirement to the parcel manager</li>
    <li>Complete land-use, technical, financial and ESG review</li>
    <li>Sign the lease or joint-venture agreement</li>
    <li>Map CDC, PEZA, DENR, fire and local permits</li>
    <li>Gate construction, equipment entry and commercial operations</li>
  </ol>
</div>

The same principle applies in New Clark City. A BCDA land agreement does not automatically deliver tax incentives. Confirm the ecozone status, eligible activity, incentive administrator and application timing separately.

## Eight questions for the first meeting

1. What is the parcel's legal name, lot number, manager and title status?
2. Who is the contracting party, and is the instrument a lease, JV or sublease?
3. What are the term, renewal, escalation and security-deposit provisions?
4. What land-use, water, power and environmental limits apply?
5. Which duties belong to CDC, CIAC, BCDA, PEZA and the local government?
6. What is the legal basis and application deadline for incentives?
7. What is the critical path for construction and operating permits?
8. What happens on exit, assignment or change of control?
