# New Clark City's 100-ha SPPI industrial park: investor guide

## Introduction

New Clark City is moving from a broad master plan toward a market with identifiable developers and competing industrial estates. Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA), the state agency that converts former base land, has leased a site to Science Park of the Philippines, Inc. (SPPI), an experienced private industrial-estate developer.

In simple terms, BCDA supplies the land while SPPI develops and operates the industrial estate. The announced PHP30 billion and 30,000 jobs include future locator activity; they are plans, not completed investment or existing employment.

## Project facts and what they mean

| Item | Publicly announced | Investor caution |
|---|---:|---|
| Development area | 100 ha; SPPI describes 106 ha | Confirm the definition of lease and net usable area |
| Land agreement | 50 years | Confirm the locator's own sublease term |
| Investment commitment | PHP30bn | Includes SPPI and future locators |
| Employment target | About 30,000 | Forward-looking across multiple activities |
| National tax estimate | PHP1.9bn | Depends on actual operations and profitability |
| Lease signing | 23 January 2025 | Plot-level completion dates are separate |

## New Clark City has multiple industrial estates

| Estate or land bank | Position | Published area |
|---|---|---:|
| Filinvest Innovation Park | Earlier private industrial park | 120 ha |
| SPPI industrial park | The 50-year BCDA lease | 100–106 ha |
| Industrial Estate 2 | Future industrial land | 1,000 ha |

These figures do not mean every hectare is serviced and immediately available. Compare the plot that can actually be delivered by your target date, utility capacity, road access, geotechnical and flood information, and investment-promotion registration.

## Seven diligence questions

1. Which plot is deliverable, and what works are included?
2. What are the sublease term, escalation and restoration obligations?
3. What firm power capacity, redundancy and renewable options are available?
4. What water, wastewater and environmental permits apply?
5. What are actual travel times to Clark airport, Subic port and SCTEX?
6. Which entity applies to PEZA, for which activity and in what sequence?
7. How large is the practical commuting and skills catchment?
