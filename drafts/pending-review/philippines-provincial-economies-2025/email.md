# English

Subject: What 115 Philippine local economies reveal about site selection

In 2025, 109 of the Philippines' 115 provincial and highly urbanized city economies expanded, and 52 grew faster than the national rate of 4.4%. Occidental Mindoro led at 11.2%.

Yet the largest economies remain concentrated in Quezon City, Makati, Laguna and Manila. This article separates growth, scale and industry clusters and shows how companies can use the PPA in location screening.
