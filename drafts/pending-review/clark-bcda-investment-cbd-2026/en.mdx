# BCDA Investment Approvals Jump 64% to ₱53.5B, Plus a New 100-Hectare Clark CBD

## Introduction

When we covered [FedEx's investment in Clark](/en/articles/clark-fedex-southeast-asia-hub-2026/), we touched on BCDA (the Bases Conversion and Development Authority) as the agency behind the airport expansion. Its own investment approval numbers are climbing fast too: BCDA approved ₱53.5 billion in investments in just the first seven months of 2025, up 64% year-on-year. Meanwhile, within Clark Freeport Zone itself, a 100-hectare new business district billed as the "BGC of Northern Luzon" is taking shape.

This article covers BCDA's overall investment trend and the new development taking shape specifically within Clark Freeport Zone.

## BCDA Investment Approvals Hit ₱53.5B in Seven Months, Up 64% Year-on-Year

<div class="space-y-6 my-8">
  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Up sharply from ₱32.7 billion a year earlier, with about 7,000 new jobs</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      BCDA approved ₱53.5 billion in investments across its economic zones in the first seven months of 2025, up 63.82% from ₱32.7 billion in the same period a year earlier. BCDA expects these investments to unlock roughly 7,000 new jobs. If the pace holds, full-year approvals could reach around ₱70 billion.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

---

## Most of That Investment Is in New Clark City (Tarlac) and Camp John Hay (Baguio)

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">A naming mix-up worth avoiding</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    One thing worth flagging: the ₱53.5 billion figure is a combined total across all the economic zones and urban development projects BCDA manages — it's not specific to Clark Freeport Zone. Investor activity has "significantly ramped up," per BCDA, particularly in New Clark City in Tarlac province and Camp John Hay in Baguio City. New Clark City has drawn contracts from Science Park of the Philippines, Sta. Clara International-Saekyung Realty, and the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), while Camp John Hay has attracted Ayala Land and Stern Real Estate, among others. It's easy to conflate "New Clark City" with "Clark," but they're distinct projects — New Clark City sits in Tarlac province, roughly 40km north of Clark Freeport Zone in Pampanga — a distinction worth keeping straight.
  </p>
</div>

## Inside Clark Freeport Zone Itself, a 100-Hectare New CBD Is Taking Shape

<div class="space-y-6 my-8">
  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Billed as the "BGC of Northern and Central Luzon"</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      Within Clark Freeport Zone in Pampanga, BCDA is leading a 100-hectare "Clark Central Business District" project, with its master plan targeted for completion within 2026. Modeled on [Bonifacio Global City (BGC)](/en/articles/clark-fedex-southeast-asia-hub-2026/) — another BCDA-led development — it's being positioned as the "BGC of Northern and Central Luzon." As a mixed commercial and lifestyle hub, its key selling point is proximity to major infrastructure: Clark International Airport, the Subic-Clark-Tarlac Expressway (SCTEX), and One Clark Boulevard.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">BCDA president: "over 170,000 jobs expected"</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      BCDA President and CEO Joshua Bingcang has said the Clark CBD is expected to "create more than 170,000 jobs." As Metro Manila's office market shows signs of saturation, this is a notable move to build out a second-city commercial and corporate alternative in Pampanga.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

## Why This Matters for Companies

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Investment momentum is building across BCDA's whole portfolio</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Combined with [FedEx, UPS, and DHL's investments in Clark](/en/articles/clark-fedex-southeast-asia-hub-2026/), BCDA's own rising investment approvals point to broader development momentum across the areas it manages, including Clark. For companies considering office or commercial space, it's worth tracking when new developments like the Clark CBD begin actively leasing or selling units. That said, New Clark City, Camp John Hay, and Clark Freeport Zone are distinct locations with different characteristics, so it's worth double-checking which specific area any given headline refers to.
  </p>
</div>
