# Philippines proposes PHP8.67bn for 67,504 DOST scholars

## Introduction

More scholarship funding is positive for companies recruiting engineers and researchers in the Philippines. It does not mean hiring will become easier next year.

The Department of Budget and Management has proposed PHP8.67 billion for Department of Science and Technology scholarship programmes in 2027, supporting 67,504 students.

The Science Education Institute manages undergraduate, graduate and specialised training. The Philippine Science High School System provides STEM-focused secondary education. In plain terms, the allocation funds a pipeline running from science high school to postgraduate study.

Two distinctions matter: the 2027 figure is a proposal, not an enacted budget, and students enter the labour market only after a multi-year lag.

  <div class="flex items-center gap-4"><span class="flex-shrink-0 w-10 h-10 bg-accent-500 rounded-full flex items-center justify-center font-bold text-navy-950">3</span><p>PHP7.55 billion goes to SEI and PHP1.12 billion to the science-high-school system.</p></div>

The policy supports talent for agriculture, health, disaster resilience, digital transformation and industry. It does not yet provide a breakdown by field, location or graduation year, nor outcomes for industry employment or retention.

## Definitions matter in science-workforce data

DOST reported 708 R&D personnel per million people for 2018. Other documents use narrower categories such as researchers or research scientists and engineers, producing different numbers.

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Do not compare unlike headcounts</h3><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">R&D personnel may include researchers, technicians and supporting staff. Scientists, researchers, R&D personnel and STEM graduates are different populations. International or time comparisons need the same year and definition.</p></div>

NAST's Pagtanaw 2050 also highlights quality, regional concentration and retention—not only the number of students. Scholarships alone cannot guarantee industrial careers without equipment, supervisors, competitive pay and credible specialist pathways.

The additional 2,407 beneficiaries will not graduate together or all join private industry. Workforce planning should track graduation cohorts by degree and region rather than use one national stock figure.

## What it means for Clark

Clark's demand spans semiconductors, aviation maintenance, logistics, IT-BPM, data centres and food technology. CDC's Town and Gown initiative creates internship links, CIAC's planned Aviation Campus targets aviation skills, and DOST scholarships support the national STEM pipeline.

They are complementary but not automatically connected. Clark employers need recruitment links extending beyond Pampanga, together with housing, transport, pay, equipment and meaningful specialist work.

## Seven checks for employers

1. Compare the enacted 2027 GAA with the proposal.
2. Obtain beneficiaries by degree, discipline and region.
3. Align graduation years with hiring campaigns.
4. Confirm scholarship service obligations and employment conditions.
5. Track internship-to-offer-to-hire-to-three-year-retention conversion.
6. Benchmark technical pay, equipment and specialist progression.
7. Design housing, commuting and family support for Clark-based roles.

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Who should pay attention</h3><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">Semiconductor, electronics, aerospace, IT, health, agritech, energy and R&D investors. Companies that create practical university projects two to four years before hiring can build stronger visibility than those waiting for graduates to enter the market.</p></div>
