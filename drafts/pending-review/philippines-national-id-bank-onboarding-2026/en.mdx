# Philippine National ID opens 211,000 bank accounts in one month

## Introduction

Bank onboarding in the Philippines is moving from uploading a photograph of an identity card toward validating a person against the government's National ID system in real time.

LANDBANK and OFBank introduced account opening through National ID Authentication Services (NIDAS) in July 2026. The Philippine Information Agency reported that more than 211,000 accounts were opened in the first month without customers presenting a physical ID.

This does not mean that identity checks were waived. It means the evidence shifted from an image of a card to information authenticated against Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) records.

## What NIDAS changes

The Philippine Identification System, or PhilSys, holds registered identity attributes such as a person's name, date of birth and facial information. With the user's consent, NIDAS enables a service provider to test whether the presented person or information matches PSA records.

LANDBANK, OFBank, PSA and the Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) signed the subscription arrangement needed to use the service. The bank can verify against the government identity record instead of treating possession of a card image as the primary evidence.

NIDAS changes how KYC evidence is produced; it does not eliminate KYC. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) instructed supervised financial institutions to accept PhilSys credentials, while Circular No. 1170 sets risk management, recordkeeping and monitoring expectations for e-KYC.

For BPO, manufacturing, hospitality and services employers in Clark, the identity rail could simplify payroll-account setup and high-volume hiring. The reported 211,000 accounts, however, cover LANDBANK and OFBank nationally and are not a Clark Freeport figure.

## Six implementation checks

1. Confirm eligibility and onboarding requirements for becoming a PSA relying party.
2. Obtain and record clear user consent for the attributes being checked.
3. Map sector regulation and AML duties that remain outside NIDAS.
4. Design fallback flows for no match, no National ID and service outages.
5. Minimise the personal data collected and retained.
6. Preserve an audit trail of the consent, result, operator and time.
