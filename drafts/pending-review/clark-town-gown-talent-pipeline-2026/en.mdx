# Clark's talent pipeline reaches 29 schools and 600+ interns

## Introduction

For a company expanding into Clark, the question after sites and permits is whether it can recruit the right people repeatedly.

Clark Development Corporation (CDC), the government administrator of Clark Freeport, and Baguio-based Saint Louis University (SLU) signed a memorandum on August 26, 2026 for CDC's Town and Gown Programme. “Town” represents the business and public community; “gown” represents academia. In plain terms, the programme connects university education with work experience in Clark.

Since its 2024 launch, the programme has placed more than 600 students and built 28 academic partnerships. SLU becomes the 29th and opens a link to Northern Luzon. Those figures show reach, not proven hiring outcomes: annual SLU capacity, eligible disciplines and conversion into permanent jobs have not been published.

## What is confirmed and what remains open

The memorandum creates a route for SLU students to gain practical exposure through CDC's programme. SLU is based in Baguio City, making the partnership a geographic expansion rather than simply another local school agreement.

## The labour-demand backdrop

Clark Freeport's employment base has expanded over time. BCDA reported 107,997 workers in 2017 and 121,341 in 2021. A CDC-related 2023 disclosure reported 138,364 workers—about 28% above 2017.

Clark's employment mix covers manufacturing, semiconductors, aviation maintenance, IT-BPM, tourism and services. In 2025, CDC and the Technical Education and Skills Development Authority (TESDA) also outlined Enterprise-Based Education and Training (EBET), placing trainees inside companies for sector-specific skills such as surface-mount assembly.

Town and Gown can be read as the university-entry layer; TESDA and EBET provide occupation-specific training. Both aim to reduce the distance between general education and tasks performed on the factory or office floor.

## Central Luzon compared with the national labour market

The Philippine Statistics Authority estimated Central Luzon's 2025 labour-force participation rate at 63.4%, below the national 64.1%. Its underemployment rate was 9.0%, below the national 11.9%. Underemployment measures workers who want more hours or additional work.

Metro Manila offers access to a much larger urban university market. Clark's differentiator is the ability to connect Pampanga institutions, Northern Luzon universities, technical training and Freeport employers. The trade-off is mobility: students coming from Baguio or other distant locations may need housing, transport and living-cost support.

## Seven diligence steps for employers

1. Split demand by occupation: engineering, quality, maintenance, IT, finance, sales and operations.
2. Match each role with course content, not only a university's name.
3. Give interns safe, confidential and measurable work rather than observation alone.
4. Budget the time of supervisors and set weekly feedback.
5. Score technical skill, attendance, communication and teamwork consistently.
6. Confirm housing, transport and allowances for students from outside Clark.
7. Track applications, starts, completions, offers, hires and one-year retention as one funnel.
