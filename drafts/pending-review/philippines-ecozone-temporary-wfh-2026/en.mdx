# Ecozone Firms Can Use Up to 90% Temporary WFH: Conditions and Tax Risk

## Introduction

The Fiscal Incentives Review Board (FIRB) has authorized investment promotion agencies (IPAs) to allow registered enterprises to use temporary work-from-home arrangements of up to 90% during the national energy emergency.

This can cover registered businesses in PEZA zones and freeports such as Clark. It is not an automatic nationwide 90% entitlement. The responsible IPA sets the ratio and conditions, while companies must manage notices, asset inventories, bonds, monthly reporting, employment and export revenue.

## Key points

<div class="bg-navy-50 border border-navy-200 rounded-lg p-8 my-12"><div class="space-y-6">
<p class="text-gray-800"><strong>1.</strong> FIRB gave IPAs authority to allow up to 90% temporary WFH.</p>
<p class="text-gray-800"><strong>2.</strong> An IPA may set a lower ceiling, but generally not below 50%.</p>
<p class="text-gray-800"><strong>3.</strong> Moving tax- and duty-free assets requires prior approval and a surety bond.</p>
<p class="text-gray-800"><strong>4.</strong> Exceeding the approved ratio can trigger regular-income-tax adjustments.</p>

FIRB does not directly grant 90% to each company. PEZA, CDC or another IPA must implement the authority. Companies should confirm whether a Letter of Authority is needed, which job groups qualify and how the ratio is calculated.

## Compliance requirements

Registered enterprises generally need to:

- Notify the responsible IPA.
- Submit an inventory of assets moved outside the zone.
- Obtain prior approval and post a surety bond for tax- and duty-free assets.
- File monthly updates on assets outside the zone.
- Maintain prescribed export revenue.
- Avoid reducing the current employee count.

If a company exceeds the approved ratio, regular income tax may apply to the average monthly excess. HR attendance data therefore needs to reconcile with tax, asset and IPA reporting.

## Ninety percent is a ceiling

The responsible IPA may set a lower ratio based on operating conditions. Information security, customer contracts and on-site equipment can also lead a company to choose less WFH.

## What it means for foreign companies

IT-BPO and shared-service operations may use the measure as an energy and transport continuity tool. Multi-site businesses and firms moving computers or other incentivized equipment face more complicated controls.

An effective implementation sequence is:

1. Verify the latest IPA notice and registration terms.
2. Document covered employees, period, calculation unit and ceiling.
3. Reconcile moved equipment against the fixed-asset register.
4. Build a joint tax, HR, IT and information-security approval flow.
5. Reconcile attendance, headcount, export revenue and asset reports monthly.

## Conclusion

Temporary WFH can strengthen business continuity, but it is also a tax and asset-control process. Companies should not treat the 90% headline as a blanket permission.
