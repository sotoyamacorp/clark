# Philippines reaches 3.7m visitors: where business demand is

## Introduction

The Philippines welcomed about 3.7 million international visitors from January through July 2026, up 4.25% year on year. The recovery is real, but it remains incomplete against 2019 and modest beside major ASEAN destinations.

For investors, the practical conclusion is that national growth does not translate evenly into every city, island, hotel or retail site.

## Recovery in 2026

| Period | Visitors | Comparison |
|---|---:|---|
| Jan-May | 2.74m | Above the 0.56m-2.56m range in the same period of 2022-2024 |
| Jan-Jun | 3.16m | About 76.5% of H1 2019's 4.13m |
| Jan-Jul | About 3.7m | +4.25% year on year |

## ASEAN comparison

| Destination | 2024 arrivals | Multiple of Philippines |
|---|---:|---:|
| Thailand | 35.55m | about 6.0x |
| Malaysia | 25.02m | about 4.2x |
| Vietnam | 17.58m | about 3.0x |
| Singapore | 16.53m | about 2.8x |
| Indonesia | 14.35m | about 2.4x |
| Philippines | 5.95m | 1.0x |

The Philippines has no land border and its destinations are geographically dispersed, so raw comparisons have limits. Even so, the gap indicates room to improve air capacity, visas, airport-to-destination transport and tourism products.

## Source-market shifts

The United States became the largest source market by mid-June, with about 591,600 arrivals. China recovered quickly after visa easing, while the formerly dominant Korean market weakened.

| Market | 2026 direction | Business implication |
|---|---|---|
| United States | Largest source market | Longer stays, family visits, English service |
| China | Rapid rebound | Payments, language and group flows |
| Korea | Slower in H1 | Reduce concentration risk |
| Japan | Recovery opportunity | Direct flights, MICE, golf and corporate travel |

## What it means for Clark

Clark can combine direct airport access, MICE, golf, family travel, business trips, Pampanga food culture and connections to New Clark City and Subic. Published early-2026 schedules showed 86 weekly seats-bearing services across four airlines between Philippine cities including Clark and Bangkok.

The region should not simply replicate Manila. Its advantage is a shorter airport transfer and a multi-destination Central Luzon product.

## Six metrics for investment decisions

1. Foreign visitors versus overseas Filipinos.
2. Airport and route seats and load factors.
3. Local room supply, occupancy and average daily rates.
4. Spend and stay length by nationality.
5. MICE, corporate and group mix.
6. Online booking and payment-method mix.
