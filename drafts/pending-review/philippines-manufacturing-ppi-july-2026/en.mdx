# Philippine factory prices rise 3.0%: a sourcing guide

## Introduction

Philippine manufacturing producer prices rose **2.983% year on year in July 2026**, normally rounded to 3.0%, the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) reported on August 28. The rate was almost unchanged from 2.980% in June but marked a clear shift from the 0.1% decline recorded in July 2025.

PPI measures prices received by domestic manufacturers at the factory gate. It is neither an import-price index nor a retail-price index, but it provides an early signal for local sourcing, contract manufacturing and price-escalation discussions.

Seasonally adjusted PPI increased 0.1% month on month after falling 0.1% in June. One month does not establish persistent acceleration. August PPI is scheduled for release on September 30.

Refined petroleum accounted for 68.6% of the slight acceleration in the headline rate. The largest contributions to total year-on-year inflation came from electronics, basic metals and transport equipment. A 3% headline does not justify a uniform 3% increase across suppliers.

## Food inflation is uneven

Food manufacturing PPI slowed to 1.3% from 1.7%. Prepared animal feeds slowed to 0.9% from 2.3%, while meat rose to 1.3%, fish processing to 4.6% and dairy to 5.4%.

Food-service and retail companies should combine the closest product series with cold-chain costs, electricity, packaging and foreign exchange.

## Why PPI is 3.0% while CPI is 6.2%

July consumer inflation was 6.2%; core inflation was 4.2%, and inflation outside NCR was 6.7%.

Freight, foreign exchange, duties, taxes, wholesale and retail margins, and companies' margin decisions sit between the two stages. Pass-through is neither one-for-one nor immediate.

## Using PPI in sourcing negotiations

1. Map the bill of materials to the closest PSA industry.
2. Check whether escalation clauses reference headline or industry PPI.
3. Track PHP/USD/JPY, freight and electricity separately.
4. Ask suppliers for the base period, index, affected cost and pass-through rate.
5. Model finished-goods price timing against inventory days.

Headline PPI should be a discussion trigger, not an automatic price-escalation formula.</p></div>
