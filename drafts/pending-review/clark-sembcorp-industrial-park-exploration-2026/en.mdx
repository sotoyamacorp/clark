# Sembcorp Explores a Clark Industrial Park: What Is and Isn't Confirmed

## Introduction

Singapore's Sembcorp Industries is exploring an industrial-park opportunity in Clark. That does not mean the project has been approved or funded. Put simply, the parties have begun discussions and inspected a possible location.

PEZA Director General Tereso Panga explicitly described the talks as exploratory. Industrial parks normally pass through site assessment, demand studies, infrastructure checks, commercial negotiation and contracting before construction begins.

## Who would do what?

Sembcorp would be the prospective developer and operator. BCDA controls and master-plans New Clark City land. PEZA administers economic-zone registration and incentives, which are not automatic. HSBC Philippines can connect international investors and financing, but no financing agreement has been disclosed.

## Sembcorp's relevant record

Sembcorp's May 2026 release put its Vietnam Singapore Industrial Park portfolio at 26 parks covering more than 14,300 hectares. Its broader urban portfolio spans more than 30 projects and 17,600 hectares in Vietnam, China and Indonesia, with over 1,200 tenants and US$64 billion in direct investment attracted.

Those numbers indicate capability, not certainty that the Vietnam model can be copied into the Philippines. Land rules, utilities, incentives, logistics and local partners differ.

## Existing New Clark City supply

Filinvest already has a 288-hectare mixed-use development aimed at light manufacturing, logistics, warehousing, cold storage and food processing. BCDA has also advanced a 100-hectare Industrial Estate 1 project. A formal Sembcorp entry would therefore need to complement, not simply duplicate, current supply—potentially through low-carbon utilities, ready-built facilities, data-centre infrastructure or tenant networks.

## What businesses should watch

Companies should not reserve budgets around an uncommitted project. Track six milestones instead: final investment decision, developer and land contract, PEZA status, power/water/connectivity specifications, logistics links, and tenant handover dates.
