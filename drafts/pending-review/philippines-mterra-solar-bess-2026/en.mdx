# MTerra Solar Targets August Operations: What Its Battery Means for Business

## Introduction

Power cost and reliability can determine the economics of manufacturing, logistics, and data centers in the Philippines. MTerra Solar, spanning Nueva Ecija and Bulacan, is one of the world's largest integrated solar and battery projects. Phase I was about 91% complete in July 2026 and was scheduled for full commercial operation in August.

## Project Scale

MTerra Solar is being developed under MGen Renewable Energy Holdings. Plans cover more than five million panels across about 3,500 hectares. Meralco reports an estimated US$4 billion investment and a 20-year power supply agreement.

| Metric | Published plan |
|---|---|
| Solar capacity | 3,500 MWp |
| Battery energy | 4,500 MWh |
| Equivalent supply | About 2.4 million households |
| Annual avoided emissions | About 4.3 million tonnes |
| Full completion target | 2027 |

Phase II started in early 2026 and targets completion in 2027. A 13-kilometer, 500-kV transmission line is planned to connect the project to the Luzon grid.

## What Battery Storage Adds

Solar output changes with daylight and weather. Batteries can store production and discharge it at another time, reducing the mismatch between output and demand. An energy capacity of 4,500 MWh could theoretically deliver 4,500 MW for one hour or 1,125 MW for four hours, but actual power, duration, and dispatch depend on the final configuration and grid requirements.

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">MW and MWh measure different things</h4><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">MW measures instantaneous power, while MWh measures stored energy. Investors should compare solar MWp, battery MW, battery MWh, and discharge duration separately.</p></div>

The government says installed storage capacity expanded from 93 MW in July 2022 to 845 MW by May 2026. MTerra is therefore part of a broader shift toward using storage to support variable renewable energy.

## Implications for Corporate Power

Potential benefits include more diversified Luzon supply, less exposure to imported fuel, and more renewable procurement options. These matter particularly to power-intensive manufacturers, semiconductor plants, cold storage, and data centers.

However, generation capacity and an individual customer's bill are not the same. The outcome depends on power supply contract prices, transmission and distribution charges, levies, market eligibility, and the company's load profile. Grid congestion, outages, and extreme weather risks also remain.

## Five Checks for Investors

1. Identify the distribution utility or economic-zone supplier for each site
2. Check eligibility for regulated supply, retail electricity, or aggregation
3. Model contracted demand, peak load, power factor, and backup needs
4. Confirm whether renewable procurement supports Scope 2 reporting
5. Budget for backup generation, on-site batteries, and business continuity

Major renewable projects show the direction of the market, but investment models should use site-specific quotations.</p></div>
