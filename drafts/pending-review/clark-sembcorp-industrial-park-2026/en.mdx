# Sembcorp explores a Clark industrial park: testing the 26-site VSIP model

## Introduction

Singapore government-linked **Sembcorp Industries** is exploring whether to develop an industrial park in the Clark area. Reports say the company met the Philippine Economic Zone Authority (PEZA), Bases Conversion and Development Authority (BCDA) and HSBC in Manila in July 2026, followed by a visit to BCDA-controlled land around New Clark City in August.

This is not an investment commitment. No specific site, area, capital budget, tenant list or timetable has been disclosed. The useful question for investors is whether Sembcorp could transfer parts of its **Vietnam Singapore Industrial Park (VSIP)** model to Clark.

## What is confirmed and what remains open

The reported July 29 meeting and subsequent site visit bring together distinct roles: PEZA handles ecozone policy and investor registration; BCDA controls land, planning and infrastructure; Sembcorp brings development and operations experience; HSBC can support financial connectivity.

## What the 26-site Vietnam model offers

Sembcorp's May 2026 announcement put the VSIP network at **26 parks and more than 14,300 hectares**. Across its urban business in Vietnam, China and Indonesia, the company reports 30 projects, more than 1,200 tenants and over US$64 billion of direct investment attracted.

The model goes beyond serviced land. Ready-built factories, logistics, power and rooftop solar can shorten start-up time and reduce initial capital needs.

## Clark already has an industrial base

PEZA said that at end-2025 Clark had **five PEZA zones, 24 locators, more than ₱62 billion in cumulative investment and over 29,000 jobs**. Filinvest Innovation Park–New Clark City also disclosed five ready-built factories totaling 12,500 square meters, 80% leased at end-2025.

The question is therefore not whether Sembcorp starts from zero. It is whether a new platform can add factory supply, integrated energy and an international tenant network to the existing Clark Freeport and New Clark City ecosystem.

## What it means for businesses

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Who should pay attention</h3><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">Electronics, precision manufacturing, food processing, logistics and data-center supply-chain companies seeking a fast start in standard facilities should watch this proposal. If realized, it could expand choices for shared infrastructure, renewable power and supplier clustering. The terms and timetable remain unknown, so it should not yet be built into an approved investment case.</p></div>

When details emerge, prospective tenants should verify:

1. PEZA eligibility and incentive conditions.
2. Floor loading, power capacity, wastewater systems and expansion space.
3. Renewable-power delivery, pricing, certificates and backup.
4. Measured routes to Clark airport, Subic port and Manila port.
5. Recruitment catchment, commuting, housing, healthcare and schools.
6. Total cost and schedule against existing industrial parks.
