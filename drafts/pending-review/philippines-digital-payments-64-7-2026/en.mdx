# Philippine Digital Payments Reach 64.7%: What Businesses Must Build

## Introduction

Digital payments accounted for 64.7% of Philippine retail transactions by volume in 2025, up from just 1.0% in 2013. Digital is now a standard channel, but the number does not mean 64.7% of payment value—or that cash has disappeared.

InstaPay leads frequency; PESONet remains important for higher-value corporate flows. One payment rail should not be forced across every use case.

## Why cash still matters

The measure is transaction volume, not value. Another 35.3% remained non-digital. Customer access, connectivity, power outages, identity checks, refunds and fees still justify multiple payment options. Cross-country rankings are also risky because definitions and measurement populations differ; the consistent BSP domestic series is more decision-useful.

## Five operating capabilities

Businesses should design channel mix by ticket size, automate order-to-payment reconciliation, set refund and erroneous-transfer rules, control fraud and access rights, and maintain an outage business-continuity process. PSA estimates the broader digital economy at PHP2.74 trillion, or 9.8% of GDP, in 2025; payments increasingly connect commerce, accounting, inventory and customer data.
