# Philippine Approved Foreign Investment Rises 68.2% to PHP115.2B in Q2

## Introduction

Approved foreign investment reached PHP115.20 billion in Q2 2026, 68.2% above Q2 2025. This is a forward-looking approval measure, not money already transferred into the Philippines.

First-half approvals totalled PHP157.84 billion, 58.0% of the PHP272.38 billion approved in all of 2025. Quarterly approvals are lumpy, so this is not a forecast.

## Where approvals went

The Netherlands led source countries with PHP50.74 billion (44.0%), followed by Germany at PHP18.05 billion and Singapore at PHP9.95 billion. Manufacturing received PHP78.81 billion (68.4%). CAR led regions with PHP55.74 billion, followed by Central Luzon at PHP36.81 billion and CALABARZON at PHP14.75 billion. These three regions captured 93.2% of approvals.

## Higher capital, fewer projected jobs

Total approved investment, foreign and Filipino, rose 73.1% to PHP541.51 billion, while projected employment fell 21.9% to 32,167. A simple ratio fell from about 13.2 projected jobs per PHP100 million in Q2 2025 to 5.9 in Q2 2026. This can reflect more capital-intensive power and manufacturing projects, not necessarily weaker quality.

## Business implications

The manufacturing concentration and Central Luzon ranking create leads for suppliers, logistics, maintenance and training providers. But companies should track named projects through construction, funding and hiring rather than treating approvals as immediate demand.
