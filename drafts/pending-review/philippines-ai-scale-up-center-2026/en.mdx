# Philippines Opens AI Scale-Up Center: Partnership Routes for Japanese Firms

## Introduction

The Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) and Converge ICT Solutions opened the AI and Scale-Up Center at the DTI Filinvest Building in Makati on August 11, 2026. The hub brings AI demonstrations, training, consultation, accelerator space and business-scaling support closer to local companies and startups.

For Japanese software, training and IT-services companies, the relevant question is not whether the Philippines is interested in AI. It is how to translate that interest into a measurable pilot for a highly varied MSME market.

## From explaining AI to applying it

Reported Center facilities include live AI demonstration areas, Accelerator Pods, pitching opportunities, training facilities and consultation space. The launch implements a November 2025 DTI-Converge memorandum, with Converge supporting connectivity and technical infrastructure.

In plain terms, the Center is designed to help companies apply AI to workflows such as inventory, customer service, billing and document processing—not only attend an awareness seminar.

Not every schedule, fee, selection criterion or foreign-partner pathway has been consolidated in a single public notice. Companies should verify each program with DTI instead of assuming the Center is an automatic sales channel.

## DTI and DOST play complementary roles

DOST is also building the AI ecosystem. DOST-ASTI reported that more than 400 researchers, public officials and MSME practitioners joined capacity-building activities in the first year of ACABAI-PH. DOST has also outlined a 26-fold expansion in high-performance computing capacity by 2028 and regional AI support for MSMEs.

## Look at adoption variance, not only market size

The PSA's 2023 establishment list counted 1,105,722 operating establishments. That is a large addressable base, but connectivity, data quality, IT staffing, budget and language operations vary widely.

DOST Caraga's AIgnite workshop focused on concrete MSME problems including inventory and customer service. This suggests that a narrow workflow pilot is more actionable than a generic AI-transformation package.

## Four partnership routes

1. **Workflow SaaS pilots** for billing, inventory, inquiry classification or forecasting
2. **Training and operating design** for managers, end users and IT staff
3. **Local resale and support** with telecom, SI and BPO partners
4. **Japan-Philippines workflow integration** for bilingual documents, quality and customer operations

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Lead with a KPI, not an AI feature</h3><p class="text-gray-700">Frame the proposal as reducing invoice-reconciliation time or shortening inquiry triage, then specify the baseline, target and measurement method. Do not promise an outcome before the pilot produces evidence.</p></div>
