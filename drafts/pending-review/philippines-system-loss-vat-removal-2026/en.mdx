# Philippines to remove VAT from system-loss charges

## Introduction

The Philippines is not abolishing the system-loss charge on electricity bills. The policy change is narrower: it is designed to **remove the 12% value-added tax from the allowable system-loss charge**.

The Energy Regulatory Commission, or ERC, approved Resolution No. 26 on August 26, 2026, treating the charge as a government-mandated pass-through cost rather than utility revenue. The Bureau of Internal Revenue, or BIR, said on September 2 that it would issue the required confirmatory circular after the publication period lapses.

Put simply, customers will continue to pay for allowable electricity losses, but they should no longer pay VAT on that amount once the BIR issuance takes effect.

## What system loss means

System loss is electricity purchased or generated but not delivered to a customer's meter. ERC separates it into two broad categories.

<div class="my-8 overflow-x-auto"><table class="w-full text-sm border-collapse">
  <thead><tr class="bg-navy-900 text-white"><th class="p-3 text-left">Category</th><th class="p-3 text-left">Examples</th><th class="p-3 text-left">Billing treatment</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr class="border-b border-navy-100 border-dashed bg-white"><td class="p-3 font-semibold">Technical</td><td class="p-3">Losses in wires, transformers and distribution equipment</td><td class="p-3">Recoverable only within ERC caps</td></tr>
    <tr class="border-b border-navy-100 border-dashed bg-navy-50"><td class="p-3 font-semibold">Non-technical</td><td class="p-3">Theft, illegal connections and metering issues</td><td class="p-3">Recoverable only within ERC caps</td></tr>
    <tr class="border-b border-navy-100 border-dashed bg-white"><td class="p-3 font-semibold">Above-cap losses</td><td class="p-3">Losses exceeding the approved rate</td><td class="p-3">Borne by the distribution utility</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table></div>

The resolution does not eliminate all system loss. It changes the tax treatment of the allowable portion collected by generation companies, the National Grid Corporation of the Philippines and distribution utilities as a pass-through item.

ERC plans to require distribution utilities to change their billing formats within 60 days of the resolution's effectivity so the non-VAT system-loss item is shown separately.

## Timing and savings

BIR said it would issue a Revenue Memorandum Circular after 15 days have elapsed from publication of ERC Resolution No. 26. Companies should therefore avoid assuming that the very next bill will automatically reflect the change. They need the BIR circular number, effectivity date and their utility's implementation month.

<div class="bg-navy-50 border border-navy-200 rounded-lg p-6 my-8 text-center">
  <p class="font-bold text-navy-950 text-lg">Monthly saving = eligible system-loss charge × 12%</p>
  <p class="text-sm text-gray-700 mt-3">If the eligible charge is ₱500, the indicative saving is ₱60.</p>
</div>

The government's illustrative estimates are ₱6 billion in annual savings nationwide and approximately ₱21 a month for a household using 200 kWh. Corporate savings will vary by consumption, utility, customer class and the system-loss rate on each bill.

BIR's RMC No. 60-2026 already excludes several government-mandated electricity items, including the Lifeline Subsidy and Green Energy Auction Allowance, from VAT. The new circular is expected to extend that framework to system loss.

## What this means for businesses

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Who should pay attention</h3><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">Factories, cold-storage facilities, data centers, hotels and large commercial properties should monitor the change. But this is not a 12% cut to the total electricity bill. It only removes VAT from one component, so companies should not treat it as a transformational reduction in project economics.</p></div>

Keep bills from before and after implementation and verify:

1. whether system loss is shown as a separate line item;
2. whether that line is excluded from the VAT base;
3. the first billing period covered and any adjustment; and
4. whether utilities implement it at different times across sites.
