# Clark Named Asia's Top MICE City: 33 Million Visitors, 85% Occupancy

## Introduction

We've mostly covered Clark's business side — BPO, aerospace, real estate investment — but there's another sector steadily building momentum there: tourism and MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences, and exhibitions). In 2025, Clark was named "Asia's Leading Meetings and Conference Destination" at the World Travel Awards — the first time a Philippine city has won in this category.

This article covers the latest data on Clark's tourism and MICE industry, and what it means for companies operating there.

## Named Asia's Leading MICE City at the World Travel Awards, a First for the Philippines

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Won in October 2025; CDC's chief called it a "defining moment"</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Clark Freeport Zone was named "Asia's Leading Meetings and Conference Destination" in the Asia & Oceania category of the 32nd World Travel Awards in October 2025 — the first time a Philippine city has won this category. Clark Development Corporation (CDC) President and CEO Agnes Devanadera called the win "a defining moment" for Clark and the Philippines' MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences, exhibitions) industry. The Philippines has won other WTA categories before (Boracay for Asia's Leading Beach Destination, among others), but this marked the country's first win specifically in the MICE category. Clark has been nominated again for the same award at the 33rd WTA in 2026, aiming to defend the title. The Department of Tourism (DOT) has positioned Clark as a primary hub for MICE, and [the modernized Clark International Airport (capacity: 8 million passengers a year)](/en/articles/clark-nscr-airport-express-2026/) is a major factor behind the growth in visitor numbers.
  </p>
</div>

## What the World Travel Awards and the Department of Tourism Actually Are

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-8">
  <div class="border-l-4 border-navy-600 bg-navy-50 p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">World Travel Awards: founded 1993, the "Oscars of travel"</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      The World Travel Awards, founded in 1993, is a private industry awards program that the Wall Street Journal has called the travel industry's equivalent of the Oscars. It runs annual gala ceremonies across world regions, with winners typically determined by votes from tourism boards and industry players in each category. In recent years its organizers have also launched sister awards in golf, ski, spa, culinary, and MICE tourism.
    </p>
  </div>
  <div class="border-l-4 border-navy-600 bg-navy-50 p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Department of Tourism (DOT): the government agency setting national tourism strategy</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      The Department of Tourism (DOT) is the Philippine government agency responsible for tourism policy and promotion nationwide. On July 15, 2026, CDC President Devanadera paid a courtesy call on Tourism Secretary Ma. Bernadita "Dita" Angara-Mathay at DOT's Makati headquarters, where both sides agreed to strengthen a "whole-of-government" push on Clark's tourism development, including MICE. DOT has identified MICE and cruise tourism as priority growth segments, with Clark counted among its key hubs.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

## Nationally, South Korea Sends the Most Visitors to the Philippines, With Japan Close Behind

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Clark-specific breakdowns by nationality aren't public</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    We haven't been able to find published data on the nationality breakdown of Clark's own visitors as of this writing. But at the national level (2025, DOT and the Bureau of Immigration), South Korea led with 1,346,301 arrivals (down 18.49% year-on-year), followed closely by the United States, with Japan, China, and Australia also among the top source markets. China trailed at 237,101 arrivals (down 14.27%). Total visitor arrivals to the Philippines reached 6,484,060 for the year (up 0.76%, including balikbayans), reflecting a gradual recovery rather than a sharp rebound. Clark is one of the country's main gateways alongside Metro Manila, so the national pattern likely applies to some degree, but confirming the actual figures will require further disclosure from CDC or DOT.
  </p>
</div>

## Hotel Occupancy Recovered From 20-30% During the Pandemic to 70-85% in 2025

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">A recovery pace that stands out regionally</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Clark's hotel occupancy, which fell to 20-30% during the pandemic, recovered to 70-85% by 2025 — a pace that reportedly outperforms many surrounding markets. Both business travel (linked to BPO and manufacturing operations) and leisure/casino tourism (including as an access point toward Baguio) appear to be supporting this occupancy recovery.
  </p>
</div>

## 1,300 New Rooms Coming 2026-2028, Including Filinvest's 5-Star Crimson Clark

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">326 rooms averaging 43 square meters each</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Driven by recovering demand, roughly 1,300 new hotel rooms are expected to open in Clark between 2026 and 2028. A flagship example: Filinvest Development's five-star Crimson Clark, a large-format property with 326 rooms averaging 43 square meters each, targeting a 2028 opening. Among existing properties, Widus Hotel — integrated with Hann Casino Resort (237 rooms, 5 dining outlets, 12 function rooms) — is directly connected to Clark Marriott and Swissôtel Clark, functioning as an interconnected resort complex.
  </p>
</div>

## SMX Clark Is Expanding Significantly, With 115 Events Held in the Past Year

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">The JCI World Congress is set to take place there in November 2026</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    The SM Group is planning to expand SMX Convention Center Clark — currently over 4,000 square meters of leasable space across three halls, three function rooms, and 14 meeting rooms — between 2026 and 2028. Once complete, it's expected to become one of SM's largest convention venues. Through an integrated approach spanning SM City Clark, Park Inn by Radisson Clark, and other properties, the area hosted 115 events over the past year (36 running events, 13 biking/duathlon/triathlon events, 9 spectator shows, 7 festivals, and more). Clark is also the only Philippine destination that holds membership in the International Congress and Convention Association (ICCA), underscoring its standing domestically for attracting international conferences. The Junior Chamber International (JCI) World Congress, drawing more than 6,000 delegates from over 120 countries and territories, is scheduled to be held in Clark from November 11-15, 2026.
  </p>
</div>

## Why This Matters for Companies

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">A base for business travel, training, and exhibitions</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    For companies already operating in Clark, or considering it, a deepening MICE ecosystem offers indirect benefits. It expands the option to hold internal training, client-facing conferences, or exhibitions locally within Clark, without needing to travel to Metro Manila. Having an airport, hotels, and convention facilities all clustered in one area translates into real cost savings for businesses that involve frequent travel and visitors.
  </p>
</div>
