# Clark Revises Residential Sublease Documents: A Guide for Foreign Occupants

## Introduction

Housing inside Clark Freeport Zone (CFZ) is not always contracted like an ordinary Philippine tenancy. A developer or lessee may hold the underlying arrangement with Clark Development Corporation (CDC) and sublease a residential unit to the occupant. CDC approval therefore becomes part of the transaction.

On 5 August 2026, CDC published revised application and documentary requirements for residential subleases. The changes matter to companies arranging expatriate housing, foreign residents and investors who plan to rent out units.

## What “Sublease” Means in Clark

CDC manages CFZ. A developer or master lessee can then sublease a residential unit to an occupant. The occupant is the sublessee; the developer or master lessee is the sublessor. CDC retains the right to approve or reject the arrangement, so submission does not guarantee approval.

## Long-Term Documentary Package

The revised document calls for two sets in a long white folder submitted to CDC's Records Management Division. Core requirements include:

1. The completed form, original notarized sublease agreement and developer endorsement
2. A primary government ID for Filipino applicants
3. For foreign nationals, the passport bio page, all Philippine stamps, and an Alien Employment Permit (AEP) and Alien Certificate of Registration (ACR) I-Card where applicable
4. The developer's undertaking confirming identity, financial and security screening
5. At least three color photographs showing the entire unit
6. Proof of CDC processing-fee and revenue-share payment and proof of contract-price payment to the developer
7. A Special Power of Attorney when a representative will sign

Foreign supporting documents need English translations. Since AEP and ACR requirements depend on the applicant's immigration and work status, companies should confirm applicability with the developer and CDC rather than assume every foreign applicant needs the same package.

## Screening Goes Beyond Identity

The developer undertaking covers financial capacity, source of funds, debt-burden ratio and proof of income. CDC lists bank statements, audited financial statements, business registrations and certificates of employment as examples of supporting evidence.

## Conditions Continue After Approval

The revised form requires registration of every occupant, including nannies and household help, and compliance with maximum bed capacity. It also permits CDC inspection upon due notice. Revenue share is paid annually for terms longer than one year and prepaid in full upon approval for terms of one year or less.

The parties have 14 calendar days from receipt of approval to sign the conforme and return all copies for a control number and dry seal. Failure makes the approval automatically null and void. Misdeclaration, unauthorized use, illegal activity or an adverse background result may trigger cancellation.

## What Companies and Investors Should Do

If HR arranges expatriate housing on its own, corporate documents, immigration records, family data and payment evidence can become fragmented. Bring HR, administration, legal and the developer into one document tracker early.

An investor intending further subleasing may also need DTI and BIR registration, a TIN, and evidence of tax filing and payment. Yield calculations should therefore include CDC revenue share, review time, vacancy during approval and a fresh process when the unit transfers.
