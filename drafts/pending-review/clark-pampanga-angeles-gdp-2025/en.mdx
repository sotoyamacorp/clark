# Pampanga GDP grows 5.6%: reading the Clark market

## Introduction

Pampanga's real GDP reached **PHP627.89 billion in 2025, up 5.6%**, according to Provincial Product Accounts released by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) on August 28, 2026. Nearby Angeles City grew 3.7% to PHP156.86 billion, while Tarlac grew 5.7% to PHP233.43 billion.

None of those figures is a stand-alone “Clark GDP.” Clark crosses provincial boundaries, while Angeles is a separate statistical unit despite its close labor, residential and consumer links. This article shows how investors can use the data without erasing those boundaries.

Pampanga's 2025 real growth included manufacturing at 7.1%, construction at 7.6%, transport and storage at 9.5%, accommodation and food at 6.8%, and real estate at 9.0%. Angeles recorded 10.9% construction growth and 9.3% information and communications growth, while manufacturing contracted 2.0%.

Manufacturing and logistics projects should therefore read a different dataset from BPO, hospitality, retail or staff-housing projects.

## Why there is no single Clark GDP

PPA follows administrative boundaries. The Clark Freeport and Special Economic Zone do not. New Clark City is on the Tarlac side; Clark International Airport and many Freeport locators are on the Pampanga side. Angeles is a nearby consumer, residential and labor center but statistically independent of Pampanga.

A practical site-selection model has three layers:

1. **Administrative location:** permits, local taxes, wages and utility connections.
2. **Labor catchment:** workforce availability within 30, 60 and 90 minutes.
3. **Sales and supply catchment:** travel time to the airport, NLEX/SCTEX, customers and suppliers.

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h3 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Who should pay attention</h3><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">Electronics, food processing and logistics companies should examine Pampanga's industrial and transport data. BPO, hotel, retail and expatriate-service operators should give more weight to Angeles' service share and per-capita output. CIAC's aviation and semiconductor ambitions are strategic context, not a substitute for project-level checks on land, power, talent and approvals.</p></div>

## Investor checklist

- Identify whether the site is on the Pampanga or Tarlac side.
- Use sector value added, not headline provincial GDP alone.
- Label any Pampanga-plus-Angeles figure as an analytical catchment.
- Do not treat GDP per capita as household income; verify wages and spending separately.
- PPA is historical, not an official forecast; build a project-specific demand case.
