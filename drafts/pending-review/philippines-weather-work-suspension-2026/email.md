# English

Subject: Philippines weather suspensions and pay: the 2026 rules

DOLE issued Labor Advisory 14-26 in August 2026 to guide private-sector work suspension during weather disturbances.

The general rules are no work, no pay; full regular pay for at least six hours worked; and proportionate regular pay below six hours. The advisory also protects workers from administrative sanctions when imminent danger prevents or causes them to refuse work.

The article covers suspension decisions, four pay scenarios, Clark-specific BCP issues and the records employers should retain.
