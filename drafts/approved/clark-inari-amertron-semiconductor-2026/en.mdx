# Malaysian Chip Giant Inari Amertron Eyes Third Clark Site, "CK3," Riding AI Demand

## Introduction

Following [our earlier overview of Clark's semiconductor export picture](/en/articles/clark-semiconductor-exports-2026/), a concrete example has emerged. Malaysian semiconductor major Inari Amertron is reportedly considering a third Clark facility, dubbed "CK3." This article covers what's behind the investment, and what it means for Japanese companies.

## The Third Site, CK3, Targets a 30% Capacity Boost and 600 New Jobs

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">The goal is scaling up production for AI, data centers, and advanced electronics</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Malaysian semiconductor major Inari Amertron Berhad is reportedly considering a third production site, "CK3," within the Clark Freeport Zone. CK3 is focused on scaling up production capacity for AI (artificial intelligence), data centers, and advanced electronics — and if it goes ahead, it's expected to boost Inari Amertron's Philippine production capacity by 30% and create 600 new jobs. It's also expected to lift annual revenue, positioning the investment as a direct response to rising AI-related demand.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="border-l-4 border-navy-600 bg-navy-50 p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Inari's flow: probe, thin and dice, connect, mold, then final-test</h4><p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">Inari specializes in turning front-end wafers into usable products. It electrically probes DC/RF performance, back-grinds the wafer, saws it into dies, connects those dies through wire bonding or flip chip, molds the package and conducts final testing. Its system-in-package work integrates multiple chips or components into one module—an increasingly important capability for AI and communications hardware.</p></div>

<p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed mb-8">Inari is therefore not the same type of company as TSMC or Samsung's wafer fabs; it sits alongside OSAT leaders ASE and Amkor. Japanese companies have lost ground in finished-chip revenue rankings but remain strong in equipment and materials through Tokyo Electron, Advantest, DISCO, Shin-Etsu and SUMCO. Clark opportunities can therefore extend beyond an OSAT plant to test tools, precision processing, chemicals, cleanrooms and maintenance services.</p>

## Who Inari Amertron Is: One of Malaysia's Largest OSAT Companies

<div class="border-l-4 border-navy-600 bg-navy-50 p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">A $2.11 billion market cap, with production sites in Malaysia, the Philippines, and China</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Inari Amertron Berhad is a technology company headquartered in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia, with OSAT (Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test) and EMS (electronics manufacturing services) as its core businesses. Its offerings span a wide range of semiconductor back-end processes, including DC/RF wafer probing, back-grinding, wafer sawing, wire bonding/flip chip, molding, and advanced SiP (system-in-package) work. The company operates 1.8 million square feet of production floor space across Malaysia, the Philippines, and China, with additional business operations in Singapore and the US. Group-wide headcount stands at 6,141, with trailing 12-month revenue of $289 million and a market cap of $2.11 billion (as of end-March 2026).
  </p>
</div>

## The Philippine Subsidiary Was Founded in 1988 and Already Employs 1,800

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">It has shipped more than 150 billion integrated circuits to date</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Inari Amertron's Philippine operations run through its Amertron Global Group subsidiaries. Amertron Philippines, Inc. was founded in 1988, and Amertron Technology in 2001 — both established players with more than a quarter-century of operating history. The Clark site already employs 1,800 people and has shipped more than 150 billion integrated circuits (ICs) worldwide to date. The proposed CK3 site represents a further expansion of this long-established, existing operation.
  </p>
</div>

## CDC, the Malaysian Embassy, and DTI-BOI All Sat In on the Investment Dialogue

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">The expansion was discussed at a July 16, 2026 meeting</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    This expansion plan was discussed at a dialogue held on July 16, 2026. Attendees included CDC (Clark Development Corporation) officials, the Embassy of Malaysia in the Philippines, DTI-BOI (Department of Trade and Industry-Board of Investments), and Inari Amertron executives — President and CEO Lau Kean Cheong and CFO Chong Poh Leng. The fact that investment promotion here goes beyond CDC alone, bringing in the embassy and national-level investment agencies, reflects how seriously the Philippine government is treating this Malaysian investment.
  </p>
</div>

## Why This Matters for Companies

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Another sign of Clark's semiconductor back-end cluster gaining depth</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Inari Amertron's expansion consideration is a concrete, company-level confirmation of [the broader growth in Clark's semiconductor exports we covered earlier](/en/articles/clark-semiconductor-exports-2026/). The fact that a company with more than 25 years of operating history is considering further expansion on the back of AI-related demand offers a useful data point for Japanese companies weighing entry into, or partnerships within, the semiconductor back-end (packaging and testing) space — a signal of how deep Clark's supply base already runs. The investment dialogue process itself, bringing together CDC, the embassy, and DTI-BOI, is also a practical reference point for which stakeholders to involve when evaluating entry.
  </p>
</div>
