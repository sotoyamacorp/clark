# Philippine Building Permits Fall 11.6% as Average Cost Rises 6.7%

## Introduction

Companies planning factories, warehouses, stores or offices in the Philippines need to separate construction demand from construction cost. PSA data for May 2026 show approved permits falling 11.6% year on year while the permit-based average cost per square meter rose 6.7%.

## Unit Cost and Materials

The PSA average rose from PHP11,974.75/sqm in May 2025 to PHP12,778.68/sqm in May 2026. Residential construction averaged PHP14,081.64/sqm and non-residential construction PHP11,390.96/sqm.

The NCR Construction Materials Wholesale Price Index (CMWPI) also accelerated.

## Why Investment Approvals Tell a Different Story

BOI approved PHP461.84 billion in H1 2026, up 21% year on year. Real-estate activities accounted for PHP36.55 billion and Central Luzon for PHP33.55 billion. Approvals indicate a future pipeline; building permits sit closer to implementation.

There is no contradiction. Approval, land acquisition, design, permitting, start and completion occur at different times and cover different universes.

## Five Budget Controls

1. Obtain a bill of quantities, not only a cost-per-square-meter estimate
2. Separate materials, equipment, labor, logistics and taxes
3. Review escalation clauses and the fixed-price validity period
4. Ring-fence contingency for design changes, FX and permit delay
5. Compare Manila, Clark and Cebu on one specification and date basis

## How to Use the Statistics

Permit data do not guarantee an actual start or completion. The average cost does not include every element of a factory budget, such as land, equipment, specialist systems, design and finance. May 2026 figures are preliminary and may be revised.
