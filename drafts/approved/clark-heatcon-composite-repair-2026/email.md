Subject: Boeing supplier establishes composite repair base in Clark

Text:
Thank you for subscribing. This is the Philippines/Clark Newsletter.

Boeing supplier Heatcon has signed a 25-year lease in Clark. The new facility will house manufacturing, materials distribution, and in-shop repair of aircraft composites.

The article explains why this is different from general aircraft maintenance, and identifies specific opportunities for Japanese companies in precision machining, inspection equipment, process consumables, logistics, and human resource development.
