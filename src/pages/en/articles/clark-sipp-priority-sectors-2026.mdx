# Clark and the 2026 SIPP: Four Priority Sectors, but Incentives Are Not Automatic

## Introduction

Locating inside an economic zone does not, by itself, guarantee a tax incentive package. A Clark project must fit an activity covered by the 2026 Strategic Investment Priority Plan (SIPP), complete registration with the proper investment promotion agency, and operate within the approved project scope.

## What the 2026 SIPP Does

The 2026 SIPP lists priority activities that may qualify for incentives under CREATE and CREATE MORE. Memorandum Order No. 47 also makes an important limitation clear: incentives apply only to the approved registered project or activity.

It is more than an industry list. The SIPP directs capital toward jobs, exports, technology transfer, and resilient supply chains. Tier I covers foundational priority activities; Tier II fills gaps in parts, processes, and import substitution; and Tier III targets structural transformation through R&D, advanced digital technology, and intellectual-property creation. Two manufacturers in the same broad industry can fall into different tiers depending on products, processes, technology, local sourcing, and export profile.

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Eligibility is not the same as approval</h4><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">A listing in the SIPP opens the door to evaluation. The final package can still depend on the activity, investment size, location, registration timing, and tier. Investors should model the project both with and without the expected incentives until approval is secured.</p></div>

## Incentives a Registered Project May Receive

| Incentive | What it may provide | Key check |
|---|---|---|
| Income Tax Holiday | Four to seven years after commercial operations begin | Duration varies by tier and location; only approved income qualifies |
| 5% Special Corporate Income Tax | Export enterprises may choose a 5% tax on defined gross income in lieu of national and local taxes for 10 years after ITH | Confirm the statutory gross-income base and excluded costs |
| Enhanced Deductions | Export enterprises may use EDR for 10 years and domestic-market enterprises for five, including qualifying extra deductions for R&D, training, labor, and power | Compare EDR with SCIT and document qualifying costs |
| Customs-duty exemption | Qualifying capital equipment, raw materials, spare parts, and accessories directly needed by the registered project | Approved use, quantities, and disposal rules matter |
| VAT relief | VAT exemption on qualifying imports and zero-rating for qualifying local purchases directly used in the registered activity | “Direct use” and supplier documentation are central |

A high-margin exporter does not automatically benefit more from SCIT, while a labor- or R&D-intensive operation may gain more from EDR. Compare total tax and compliance cost over the full incentive period.

## Four Sectors That Fit Clark

### 1. Semiconductors and electronics

Tier I covers manufacturing, assembly, packaging, testing, and related semiconductor and electronics services. Texas Instruments operates a semiconductor facility in Clark Freeport, illustrating the existing base for testing, materials, precision parts, and equipment support suppliers.

### 2. Aircraft MRO

Maintenance, repair, and overhaul of aircraft and components is explicitly included. SIA Engineering (Philippines) already performs heavy maintenance in Clark, while Lufthansa Technik Philippines plans a new facility for up to nine widebody aircraft, with operations targeted for 2028.

### 3. Integrated logistics

The plan covers transport infrastructure and integrated logistics combining warehousing, digital inventory, packaging, order processing, and last-mile services. UPS's new Clark airport hub, targeted for full operation in 2026, is a concrete example. A passive warehouse is not automatically equivalent to an integrated logistics project.

### 4. Data centers and digital infrastructure

Tier III identifies AI, cybersecurity, and data-center facilities among activities critical to structural transformation. PLDT Group's VITRO Clark and NTT's Clark Data Center are already operating locally. Power reliability, redundant connectivity, land, cooling, water, and talent all need to be assessed together.

## A Practical Clark Registration Sequence

Match those functions to the SIPP, then ask CDC's Business One-Stop Shop or qualified advisers about the registration category, permits, and timeline. This reduces the risk of selecting a site that does not fit the intended registration.</p></div>

1. Define the entity's activities and revenue streams
2. Map them to a 2026 SIPP activity and potential tier
3. Separate CDC registration from sector-specific and local permits
4. Compare business cases with and without incentives
