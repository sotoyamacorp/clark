# Clark Auto-Renews Business Permits for 220 Locators: A Compliance Guide

## Introduction

Business-permit renewal is a continuity issue for every Clark Freeport locator. In 2026, Clark Development Corporation (CDC), the Freeport's estate manager and investment-promotion agency, introduced automatic annual renewal for qualified locators. The initial group covered 220 companies.

## What Changed

In plain terms, qualified companies no longer need to restart the same permit application each cycle and wait for approval. CDC renews the permit annually when the locator remains in good standing.

| Item | Previous process | 2026 model |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle | Reported three-year manual renewal | Annual automatic renewal |
| Company action | Apply, submit and wait | Maintain eligibility and confirm CDC notice |
| Coverage | Companies due for renewal | Qualified locators in good standing |
| Initial group | Not disclosed | 220 locators |
| Main benefit | Permit review | Lower compliance cost and uncertainty |

Republic Act No. 11032, the Ease of Doing Business law, applies to government-owned corporations and requires agencies to simplify transactions and publish requirements, timing and fees in Citizen's Charters. CDC's measure is a Clark-specific implementation of this broader national policy.

## Eligibility and Remaining Unknowns

CDC publicly described the eligible group as businesses “in good standing.” That normally means no material unresolved issues involving permits, contracts, reports or payments. Public materials reviewed for this article do not yet provide the complete automatic screening checklist, notice timing or cure period.

Confirm the latest Citizen's Charter, eBAR notice and responsible CDC department before relying on automatic renewal.</p></div>

## Six Annual Controls for Japanese Companies

1. Keep the legal name, address, officers and contacts current in eBAR
2. Clear all CDC periodic and incentive-related reporting obligations
3. Review lease, sublease, insurance, deposit and payment status
4. Track building, fire, labor-safety, environmental and sanitation permits
5. Monitor import/export and bring-in/bring-out compliance
6. Archive the eligibility notice, renewed permit and any exception notice

## How Clark Differs from an LGU Permit

In Manila and other ordinary jurisdictions, businesses renew local permits through a city or municipal Business Permit and Licensing System. In Clark, CDC acts as both estate manager and investment-promotion agency and administers locator permissions inside the Freeport.

The reform is therefore not a nationwide automatic-renewal program. Separate registrations and permits from the BIR, SEC, DOLE, DENR, BFP and other agencies may still apply and are not automatically renewed by CDC.

## CDC, PEZA and SBMA Compared: Speed and Digitalization of Permitting

Clark's automatic renewal is not an isolated CDC initiative. Several Philippine investment promotion agencies (IPAs) are pursuing their own permitting digitalization, and this reform extends an area where CDC has already been ahead of peers. Here we compare the three main freeport/ecozone-operating agencies—CDC (Clark), PEZA (Philippine Economic Zone Authority, which oversees ecozones nationwide) and SBMA (Subic Bay Metropolitan Authority)—on online application availability and renewal mechanics.

| Item | CDC (Clark) | PEZA (nationwide ecozones) | SBMA (Subic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Status | BCDA subsidiary; estate manager and IPA | Independent agency overseeing 430+ ecozones nationwide | BCDA-affiliated; operator of Subic Bay Freeport |
| Main online system | eBAR (registration, permits and incentive processing combined) | eARS (registration application), PTOPS/BERMS (e-payment, certificate issuance) | SBFBRS (e-subicbay.com; online registration and renewal) |
| Permit renewal model (as of August 2026) | Qualified locators renew automatically each year (introduced 2026; 220 companies at launch) | Renewal generally requires annual document submission and review; no automatic-renewal announcement found as of this writing | Online filing is available, but renewal still follows the standard review process, and applicants are advised to start two months before expiry |
| Third-party recognition | FIRB (Fiscal Incentives Review Board) said in December 2025 that CDC is the first IPA with a system like eBAR and recommended other IPAs adopt it; FIRB also cited CDC as the only IPA using all modules of FIRMS | No equivalent third-party recognition found in the sources reviewed for this article | Same as PEZA (unconfirmed) |

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">A flat read of CDC's strengths and weaknesses</h4><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">CDC's strength is procedural: it built eBAR ahead of peers, and FIRB has recognized it more than once. Its weakness is scale and choice. PEZA offers far more location options across 430-plus ecozones, and BOI is not tied to any zone at all. If your business does not fit the sectors concentrated in Clark—manufacturing, IT-BPO and similar—procedural convenience alone is not a reason to choose CDC.</p></div>

We also cover BOI here for completeness. Unlike CDC, PEZA and SBMA, the Board of Investments (BOI) is not tied to any specific zone: any project on the Strategic Investment Priority Plan (SIPP) can register with BOI regardless of location. Per the BOI Citizen's Charter (2025, 2nd Edition), standard processing runs 10 to 20 working days depending on company size and activity. We found no public evidence of a CDC-style automatic annual renewal at BOI. However, the CREATE MORE Act, effective November 2024, largely harmonized major incentive terms—including extended income tax holiday caps—across BOI, PEZA, CDC and other IPAs. As a result, the choice between agencies increasingly comes down to which agency's process fits your operations, rather than which offers better incentives.

## The Bigger Picture: Philippines' Ease of Doing Business Reforms

CDC's automatic renewal is part of a broader nationwide EODB push. Understanding that context helps when evaluating sites outside Clark, or when assessing whether other agencies might adopt similar measures.

### The baseline set by RA 11032

Republic Act No. 11032 (the Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act), in force since 2018, requires government agencies and GOCCs to process simple transactions within 3 working days, complex transactions within 7 working days, and highly technical transactions within 20 working days. Applications not decided within the deadline—absent a valid written justification—are deemed automatically approved. CDC, as a government-owned corporation, falls under this framework, and the automatic-renewal program fits within it.

### What's ongoing or planned

In May 2026, ARTA held its "Ease of Doing Business Month" (an annual May observance under Presidential Proclamation No. 818) and co-hosted a Business Ready (B-READY) Reforms Forum with the World Bank Group and GSIS. In the World Bank's 2025 B-READY report, the Philippines ranked 53rd of 101 economies (overall score 61.04, with a stronger 26th-place showing in the regulatory framework category), and the government has made climbing this ranking an ongoing policy goal. ARTA has signaled plans to expand the Philippine Business Hub to more LGUs, though a nationwide rollout date could not be confirmed as of this writing.

National reform and each agency's individual initiatives do not necessarily move at the same pace, so it is worth confirming current practice at each site under consideration.</p></div>

## Implication for Location Decisions

Covering 220 locators suggests a tangible reduction in administrative cost for a meaningful group of existing companies. New investors should still compare the full path—initial registration, land and building contracts, construction, import/export and incentives—not automatic renewal alone.

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8"><h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Shift the KPI from expiry to eligibility</h4><p class="text-gray-700 leading-relaxed">Instead of only filing 90 days before expiry, track overdue reports, expired supporting permits, contractual breaches and unpaid balances monthly. That is the operating discipline behind the reform's benefit.</p></div>
