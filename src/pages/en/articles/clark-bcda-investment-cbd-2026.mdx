# BCDA Investment Approvals Jump 64% to ₱53.5B, Plus a New 100-Hectare Clark CBD

## Introduction

When we covered [FedEx's investment in Clark](/en/articles/clark-fedex-southeast-asia-hub-2026/), we touched on BCDA (the Bases Conversion and Development Authority) as the agency behind the airport expansion. Its own investment approval numbers are climbing fast too: BCDA approved ₱53.5 billion in investments in just the first seven months of 2025, up 64% year-on-year. Meanwhile, within Clark Freeport Zone itself, a 100-hectare new business district billed as the "BGC of Northern Luzon" is taking shape.

This article covers BCDA's overall investment trend and the new development taking shape specifically within Clark Freeport Zone.

## BCDA Investment Approvals Hit ₱53.5B in Seven Months, Up 64% Year-on-Year

<div class="space-y-6 my-8">
  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Up sharply from ₱32.7 billion a year earlier, with about 7,000 new jobs</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      BCDA approved ₱53.5 billion in investments across its economic zones in the first seven months of 2025, up 63.82% from ₱32.7 billion in the same period a year earlier. BCDA expects these investments to unlock roughly 7,000 new jobs. If the pace holds, full-year approvals could reach around ₱70 billion.

<p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed mb-8">
Both areas sit under the same broad "Clark Special Economic Zone" umbrella, but they're in different provinces and different scales. Clark Freeport Zone spans 4,400 hectares around Angeles City and Mabalacat in Pampanga province (2,200 hectares managed by the Clark Development Corporation, plus 2,200 hectares occupied by Clark International Airport). New Clark City, its northern neighbor across the border in Tarlac province (Capas and Bamban), spans 9,450 hectares — more than double the area. The two are connected by the Subic-Clark-Tarlac Expressway (SCTEX).
</p>

## What Are the Specific Projects Behind This Round of Approvals?

    <ul class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed space-y-2 list-disc list-inside">
      <li><strong>Science Park of the Philippines, Inc.</strong> — A major industrial park/ecozone developer; likely an industrial land project.</li>

      <li><strong>Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP)</strong> — BSP already operates a mint and security printing complex in New Clark City; this investment appears to be a related expansion.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

      <li><strong>Stern Real Estate</strong> — A real estate development.</li>
      <li><strong>Top Taste and Trading, Inc. / Amare La Cucina / Prime Collective Corp.</strong> — F&B and retail tenant investments, likely tapping Baguio's popularity as a highland tourist destination.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed mb-8">
BCDA hasn't disclosed a detailed breakdown of investment amounts per contract, but a pattern emerges: New Clark City's approvals skew toward industrial land, infrastructure, and financial facilities, while Camp John Hay's skew toward commercial, residential, and F&B — consumer-facing uses. BCDA hasn't broken out how much of the ₱53.5 billion, if any, is tied to specific projects within Clark Freeport Zone itself; we cover the zone's flagship project, the Clark CBD, separately below.
</p>

## Inside Clark Freeport Zone Itself, a 100-Hectare New CBD Is Taking Shape

<div class="space-y-6 my-8">
  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Billed as the "BGC of Northern and Central Luzon"</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      Within Clark Freeport Zone in Pampanga, BCDA is leading a 100-hectare "Clark Central Business District" project, with its master plan targeted for completion within 2026. Modeled on [Bonifacio Global City (BGC)](/en/articles/clark-fedex-southeast-asia-hub-2026/) — another BCDA-led development — it's being positioned as the "BGC of Northern and Central Luzon." As a mixed commercial and lifestyle hub, its key selling point is proximity to major infrastructure: Clark International Airport, the Subic-Clark-Tarlac Expressway (SCTEX), and One Clark Boulevard.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">BCDA president: "over 170,000 jobs expected"</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      BCDA President and CEO Joshua Bingcang has said the Clark CBD is expected to "create more than 170,000 jobs." As Metro Manila's office market shows signs of saturation, this is a notable move to build out a second-city commercial and corporate alternative in Pampanga.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

## BGC vs. This "Second BGC": How BCDA's Role Differs

<div class="space-y-6 my-8">
  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">At BGC, BCDA was a 45%-stake joint venture partner</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      BGC's development traces back to 1994, when President Ramos approved the privatization of the 440-hectare former Fort Bonifacio base. In February 1995, BCDA formed a joint venture, Fort Bonifacio Development Corporation (FBDC), with a consortium led by Metro Pacific, handing over development of 150 hectares. The ownership split was 55% private, 45% BCDA. In 2003, Ayala Corporation (through Ayala Land) and the Campos Group's Evergreen Holdings bought Metro Pacific's controlling stake, and Ayala Land has led FBDC's master planning and day-to-day development ever since. In effect, BCDA held the land but ceded operational control to a private-majority joint venture — a minority-stakeholder role.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">For the Clark CBD, BCDA is currently leading directly</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      For the Clark CBD, based on what's been publicly disclosed as of August 2026, no BGC-style joint venture with a private-majority partner (like FBDC) has been announced yet. BCDA itself is described as playing "the pivotal/leading role," and at the master-planning stage it's operating as the direct lead. That's a different pattern from New Clark City's Filinvest joint venture (Filinvest Innovation Park) or Clark Global City, where BCDA sold 177 hectares within Clark Freeport Zone outright to Udenna's Global Gateway Development Corporation (GGDC) to lead development. Whether a BGC-style private joint venture or sale eventually gets announced for the Clark CBD is worth watching as a signal of how fast the project will move.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

## Why This Matters for Companies

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Investment momentum is building across BCDA's whole portfolio</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Combined with [FedEx, UPS, and DHL's investments in Clark](/en/articles/clark-fedex-southeast-asia-hub-2026/), BCDA's own rising investment approvals point to broader development momentum across the areas it manages, including Clark. For companies considering office or commercial space, it's worth tracking when new developments like the Clark CBD begin actively leasing or selling units. That said, New Clark City, Camp John Hay, and Clark Freeport Zone are distinct locations with different characteristics, so it's worth double-checking which specific area any given headline refers to.
  </p>
</div>
