# BIR's E-Invoicing Mandate (EIS) Takes Full Effect by End of 2026: What Japanese Companies Should Prepare Now

## Introduction

"I heard the Philippines is rolling out mandatory e-invoicing, but I'm not sure what we need to do or by when." We hear this a lot from Japanese companies running accounting operations in the Philippines.

The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) is moving forward with its Electronic Invoicing System (EIS) mandate, with a compliance deadline of December 31, 2026. This article breaks down what the system involves and what companies should start preparing now.

## The EIS framework and its legal basis

### What is the BIR, exactly?

The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) is the Philippine government agency responsible for national tax collection, operating under the Department of Finance (DOF). It's the Philippine equivalent of Japan's National Tax Agency, handling the assessment and collection of income tax and VAT, tax audits, and — relevant here — setting tax-related regulations like the e-invoicing mandate. Any company doing business in the Philippines must register with the BIR (obtaining a Tax Identification Number, or TIN) after incorporating, and the BIR is the agency you'll deal with for day-to-day tax matters.

The Electronic Invoicing System (EIS) was formalized under Revenue Regulations No. 11-2025, issued by the BIR on February 27, 2025. It took effect on March 14, 2025, and is grounded in Sections 237 and 237-A of the National Internal Revenue Code, as amended by the CREATE MORE Act.

### How the rules evolved

  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">1. RR 11-2025 establishes the framework (February 2025)</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      This set out the requirement to issue structured (JSON-format) electronic invoices and transmit sales data to the BIR's EIS. The original compliance deadline was March 14, 2026.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">2. RR 26-2025 extends the deadline (October 2025)</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      The compliance deadline for issuing e-invoices was extended to December 31, 2026.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">3. A phased rollout</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      Phase 1 covers large taxpayers, e-commerce businesses, and companies already using computerized accounting systems. Phase 2 is expected to expand coverage to exporters and POS-based retailers.
    </p>
  </div>

## Who's covered and the technical requirements

<div class="grid grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-2 gap-6 my-12">
  <div class="bg-white border-2 border-gray-200 rounded-lg p-6 shadow-sm">
    <div class="text-3xl mb-3">🏢</div>
    <h4 class="text-base font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Who's covered</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-gray-700 mb-4">
      E-commerce businesses, companies registered under BIR's Large Taxpayers Service (LTS), and companies already using computerized accounting or invoicing systems.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="bg-white border-2 border-gray-200 rounded-lg p-6 shadow-sm">
    <div class="text-3xl mb-3">📄</div>
    <h4 class="text-base font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Documents covered</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-gray-700 mb-4">
      Sales invoices, official receipts, service invoices, and debit/credit notes are all in scope.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="bg-white border-2 border-gray-200 rounded-lg p-6 shadow-sm">
    <div class="text-3xl mb-3">⚙️</div>
    <h4 class="text-base font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Technical requirements</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-gray-700 mb-4">
      Invoices must be issued in structured JSON format through a BIR-registered/accredited system and transmitted to the EIS within 3 days of the transaction.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="bg-white border-2 border-gray-200 rounded-lg p-6 shadow-sm">
    <div class="text-3xl mb-3">🔑</div>
    <h4 class="text-base font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Registration process</h4>
    <p class="text-sm text-gray-700 mb-4">
      Companies need to register and get accredited through the EIS certification portal (eis-cert.bir.gov.ph), then obtain a Permit to Transmit before issuing e-invoices.
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

## What this means for Japanese companies

**The first step is figuring out whether your company falls within scope.** If you run an e-commerce operation in the Philippines, have been designated a large taxpayer, or already use a computerized accounting system, you likely have a high-priority reason to act on this now.

Getting compliant takes real lead time — adapting your invoicing system to output JSON, coordinating with a BIR-accredited system vendor, and reworking internal accounting workflows all take planning. With roughly 5 months left until the December 31, 2026 deadline, if there's a chance your company is in scope, we'd recommend consulting a local tax and IT specialist soon and mapping out a compliance timeline.
