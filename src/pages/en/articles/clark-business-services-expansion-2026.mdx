# Clark's Professional Services and BPO Base Deepens as Two Firms Expand

## Introduction

"Who do we even hire for accounting and tax in Clark?" "How easy is it to staff a BPO team there?" These are practical questions we hear often from companies seriously weighing a Clark expansion. In July 2026, two developments answered them almost simultaneously: accounting firm P&A Grant Thornton opened a new office, and BPO giants MicroSourcing and Beepo opened a joint facility.

This article covers both moves and what they say about how well-supported companies operating in Clark actually are.

## P&A Grant Thornton Opens a New Clark Office

On June 26, 2026, major accounting firm P&A Grant Thornton opened a new office in Clark Freeport Zone.

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Who is P&A Grant Thornton?</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Founded in 1988 by Ben Punongbayan and Jose Araullo, P&A Grant Thornton is a major professional services firm with over 30 years of history in the Philippines. It employs more than 1,500 people and has previously operated offices in Makati, Davao, Cebu, and Cavite. Its services span audit, tax, advisory, and outsourcing.
  </p>
</div>

<p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed mb-8">
Co-founder Jose Araullo said at the opening ceremony: "When Ben Punongbayan and I started the firm in 1988, we inculcated our work with dedication and integrity." Chairman and Managing Partner Romualdo V. Murcia III added: "Today is more than the opening of a new office. It's a meaningful step forward in our journey as a firm." The firm cited positioning itself closer to one of the country's fastest-growing investment corridors and extending its service reach across Central and Northern Luzon as its rationale for the move.
</p>

## MicroSourcing and Beepo Open a Joint 400-Seat Facility

Around the same time professional services were expanding, a major move happened on the BPO (business process outsourcing) side too.

<div class="space-y-6 my-8">
  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">1. Opened July 29, 2026, at Clark City Front Mall</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      MicroSourcing and Beepo, both major BPO providers, jointly opened a 750-square-meter service delivery center on the 6th floor of Tower 2 at Clark City Front Mall, within Clark Freeport Zone.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">2. Seats up to 400, covering back office to healthcare, finance, and tech</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      The new site can seat up to 400 professionals at once, supporting services ranging from back-office operations to healthcare, finance, and technology. Both companies expect the site to reach full capacity by year-end, with further expansion already under evaluation.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm">
    <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">3. Part of a broader shift toward decentralizing IT-BPM outside Manila</h4>
    <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
      Consolidated revenues from Philippine-based IT-BPM firms were projected to exceed $40 billion (roughly ₱2.48 trillion) for full-year 2025. As outsourcing demand grows, firms are building larger regional operations to access skilled talent outside the capital while reducing concentration risk. CDC Vice President Noelle Mina D. Meneses said the investment "reinforces Clark's position as one of the country's fastest-growing business and investment destinations."
    </p>
  </div>
</div>

## What Both Moves Together Say About Clark's Business Maturity

At first glance, P&A Grant Thornton's new office and MicroSourcing/Beepo's expansion look like unrelated news items. Put side by side, though, they show Clark's business environment deepening on two fronts at once: the "building" side and the "supporting" side.

<p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed mb-8">
Stories we've covered before — like <a href="/en/articles/clark-digital-infrastructure-hub-2026/" class="text-accent-700 underline hover:text-accent-500">data center investment</a> and the <a href="/en/articles/clark-aerodistrict-aerospace-hub-2026/" class="text-accent-700 underline hover:text-accent-500">aerospace industry cluster plan</a> — represent the "building" side (manufacturing and infrastructure). This month's P&A Grant Thornton and MicroSourcing/Beepo moves represent the "supporting" side — the accounting, tax, and BPO talent that lets those industries actually operate. The fact that both are expanding in parallel is a real signal of how mature the Clark market is becoming.
</p>

## Why This Matters for Companies

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">For Japanese companies weighing a Clark expansion</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    "We want to expand, but we're not sure we can find a local accounting/tax partner or back-office staff" is a concern we hear often in the early stages of expansion planning. The presence of a major professional services firm like P&A Grant Thornton with a Clark office directly addresses that concern. Large BPO facilities like MicroSourcing and Beepo's are also an indirect signal of the depth of English-speaking office talent available in the wider region. For companies considering a footprint that isn't entirely concentrated in Manila, this is a trend worth continuing to track.
  </p>
</div>
