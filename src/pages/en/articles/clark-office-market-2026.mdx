# Clark's Office Market: Vacancy Falls to 19.8% as BPO Majors Keep Moving In

## Introduction

We recently covered [BCDA's rising investment approvals and the Clark CBD plan](/en/articles/clark-bcda-investment-cbd-2026/), but actual tenant demand in Clark's office market is also steadily improving. Pampanga's office vacancy rate has dropped from 24.4% to 19.8% in a year, with BPO majors like Sutherland, DXC Technology, and IntouchCX all expanding their footprints.

This article covers the latest data on Clark-centered Pampanga's office market, and what it means for companies considering a presence there.

## Vacancy Improved From 24.4% to 19.8%, on Total Stock of 538,200 sqm

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">A 4.6-percentage-point improvement in a year</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    According to real estate firm Colliers, Pampanga's office vacancy rate stood at 19.8% as of the nine months through September 2025 — down 4.6 percentage points from 24.4% a year earlier. Pampanga's total office stock is 538,200 square meters, with the majority concentrated in Clark Freeport Zone. Proximity to Metro Manila, international connectivity via Clark International Airport, and mature infrastructure continue to underpin tenant demand.
  </p>
</div>

---

## BPO Majors — Sutherland, DXC, IntouchCX — Keep Expanding

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">Year-to-date take-up is already nearing last year's full-year total</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    Major BPO companies — Sutherland, DXC Technology, IntouchCX, NAC Global, and KMC Solutions — have all recently expanded their Pampanga footprints. As a result, year-to-date net office take-up has reached 33,000 square meters, nearing the full-year 2024 figure of 40,700 square meters. Combined with [the accounting firm P&A Grant Thornton's and MicroSourcing's expansions we covered earlier](/en/articles/clark-business-services-expansion-2026/), it's clear tenant demand for Clark is strengthening simultaneously across multiple segments — professional services and BPO alike.
  </p>
</div>

Comparing purely within the Philippines, Clark's cost advantage is clear.

Per Knight Frank, Manila's prime office rent ranks 4th-cheapest among 23 Asia-Pacific markets (Q4 2025, $29.04/sqft/year) — cheaper than Phnom Penh, though pricier than Kuala Lumpur and Jakarta. That's still under a quarter of Singapore's rate ($125.66) and far below Hong Kong's ($155.52). Layering "the cheapest spot within the Philippines" (Clark) on top of "a relatively cheap country within the region" (the Philippines) makes the cumulative cost advantage easier to picture.

## 86,000 sqm of New Supply Is Planned for 2026-2029

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">SM, Aeropark, and Megaworld are behind the pipeline</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    In response to rising demand, the supply side is also moving. Between 2026 and 2029, major developers SM, Aeropark, and Megaworld are planning a combined 86,000 square meters of new office development, centered on Clark and San Fernando. If demand growth continues to outpace supply, vacancy could keep falling from here, with the potential for upward pressure on rents.
  </p>
</div>

## Why This Matters for Companies

<div class="border-l-4 border-accent-500 bg-white p-6 shadow-sm my-8">
  <h4 class="font-bold text-gray-900 mb-3">A window of choice on office space that may not last</h4>
  <p class="text-gray-700 text-sm leading-relaxed">
    For companies considering an office presence in Clark, there's still a good range of options available today — but given how quickly vacancy has been improving and the timing of upcoming new supply, now may be the window to lock in favorable properties and rents. The current strength of tenant demand, particularly from BPO and professional services, is worth factoring into any office strategy discussion.
  </p>
</div>
